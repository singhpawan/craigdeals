
This PI, we're managing two critical priorities in parallel: ensuring platform stability during a planned certificate upgrade and accelerating our data monetization roadmap. We've proven our DPaaS decommissioning playbook at scale (250+ products, 3.5PB in 3 months), and we're now enabling product teams to execute independently while expanding partnership capabilities with Equifax, Cognizant, and wealth management clients. By building composable frameworks—automated provisioning, ingestion, and observability

PI Readout: "Scale Through Frameworks, Enable Through Infrastructure, Secure at Every Layer"
Objective 1: Operational Continuity & Security
Keep the platform stable and secure while strengthening our compliance posture
* DPaaS Certificate Upgrade (Feb 17th) - managed 2-hour window, ~10min actual downtime
* Splunk + Qradar SIEM integration (Big ID PoC validated)
* Service user password deprecation (aligned with last year's human user deprecation)
* BC/DR strategy for region failures
* Compliance collaboration with Ops team on governance standards
Objective 2: Product Enablement at Scale
Expand data monetization and partnership capabilities through proven playbooks
* DPaaS Migration Program: Scale WP playbook (250+ products, 3.5PB) → Enable Debit, Loyalty, Falcon teams to self-serve
* Data Sharing Expansion: Equifax, Cognizant, Georgia Banking, TRP, Ethos (building on Mastercard, UBS, FHB relationships)
* Card Pro team onboarding to CDP Snowflake (new consumption pattern)
* Product team training & self-service enablement
* Partner integration success metrics & revenue tracking
Objective 3: Platform Frameworks for the Data Lifecycle
Build composable, reusable infrastructure so product teams scale independently

* Data Movement Layer: Automated ingestion framework + tagging ontology definition with Platform team
* Observability Layer: Enhanced monitoring + alerting framework (move beyond alerts-only)
* Network Integration Redesign: Tenant migration support + ADR updates
* Framework adoption & self-serve metrics
Objective 4: Dual Platform Support & Transition
Ensure seamless migration from legacy to new platform with zero disruption
* Legacy platform maintenance & certificate management
