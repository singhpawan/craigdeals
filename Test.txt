## Risk Factors & Assumptions

### High-Risk Items
- **PCI DSS compliance complexity** - May require additional 40-80 hours
- **Legacy AD integration challenges** - Could add 20-40 hours
- **Data sensitivity classification** - May need 16-32 additional hours
- **Network security requirements** - Could require 12-24 extra hours

### Assumptions
- Existing Snowflake enterprise account
- Active Directory already configured
- Basic data governance framework in place
- Dedicated project team availability
- Standard PCI DSS Level 1 requirements

### Dependencies
- Business requirements finalized
- Data classification completed
- Network access approvals
- Vendor contract negotiations
- Security tool availability
